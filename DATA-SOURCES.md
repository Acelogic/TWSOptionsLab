# Market data sources

Options Lab connects to a locally signed-in TWS or IB Gateway. Instrument coverage, subscription type, historical availability and account permissions determine which fields the browser can display.

| Feature | Source and interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Instrument directory and chains | IBKR returns listings, trading classes, prospective expirations, strike sets and contract multipliers. The server qualifies individual option series before using their contract IDs. Futures options are tied to a futures contract and its exchange-specific terms. [Option chains](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/tws-api/doc/contracts/option-chains) · [Futures options](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/tws-api/contracts/futures-options) |
| Quotes and feed mode | TWS identifies the delivered subscription as live, frozen, delayed or delayed-frozen. The browser displays the broker’s mode and uses two-sided quotes where the selected calculation requires them. Watchlists and API clients share the account’s market-data capacity. [Market data types](https://interactivebrokers.github.io/tws-api/market_data_type.html) · [Market data lines](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/general/market-data-subscriptions/market-data-lines/introduction) |
| Previous closes | A TWS close tick gives the last available previous-day closing price. Its callback time is the receipt time. For compatible instruments, Options Lab estimates the previous regular-session cutoff from contract liquid hours and the supported US exchange calendar. A stale callback crossing into a new prior session loses that attribution. US equity closes from TWS can reflect corporate-action adjustments. [IBKR tick definitions](https://interactivebrokers.github.io/tws-api/tick_types.html) · [NYSE market calendar](https://www.nyse.com/trade/hours-calendars) |
| IV, Greeks, volume and open interest | The broker’s option computation can supply IV, Greeks and the model underlying. The builder identifies price-pair estimates separately. Volume and reported open interest have their own broker fields. Option and underlying entitlements affect coverage. [Option Greeks](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/tws-api/doc/md/option-greeks) · [Streaming tick definitions](https://interactivebrokers.github.io/tws-api/tick_types.html) |
| Historical charts | Per-contract intraday bars and historical time and sales are subject to broker availability and pacing. IBKR identifies expired options and futures options, option end-of-day data and native combo history among its limitations. Locally recorded marks can preserve observations made while a contract was available. [Historical data limits](https://interactivebrokers.github.io/tws-api/historical_limitations.html) · [Historical time and sales](https://interactivebrokers.github.io/tws-api/historical_time_and_sales.html) |
| Flow, news and events | The activity view displays updates and historical prints for selected contracts. Option live tick-by-tick coverage differs from the historical interface. News and Wall Street Horizon events depend on enabled providers, contract coverage and entitlements. [Tick-by-tick coverage](https://interactivebrokers.github.io/tws-api/tick_data.html) · [News](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/tws-api/doc/news/providers) · [Wall Street Horizon](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/tws-api/doc/wall-street-horizon/event-data) |
| Brokerage history | TWS supplies its available execution window and current positions. Official Activity Flex XML can backfill older executions, closed lots and lifecycle bookings. Options Lab stores imported records in the local account-scoped ledger. Independent option-leg cash results, broker FIFO figures and saved modeled returns are labeled separately. [Execution data](https://interactivebrokers.github.io/tws-api/executions_commissions.html) · [Activity Flex reference](https://www.ibkrguides.com/reportingreference/reportguide/activity%20flex%20query%20reference.htm) |
